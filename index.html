About me

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science (AI/ML) at the University at Buffalo. I like turning raw images and sensor data into models that do something useful in the real world.

My path into AI started during my bachelor's in Computer Science (AI/ML) in Hyderabad. A data science internship got me building and comparing classic models for fraud detection, fare prediction and vaccination forecasting. From there I moved into deep learning and computer vision: real-time sign language recognition with MediaPipe, a U-Net that removes shadows from photos, and a YOLOv8 detector that finds litter in cluttered scenes.

I care about the whole pipeline, not just the model. That means collecting and cleaning data, choosing the right metrics, training carefully, and then shipping a demo people can try. Most of my projects end with a Gradio or Flask app for exactly that reason.

As a graduate research assistant on the ASAP project, I work with a multi-sensor rig of hyperspectral, RGB and thermal cameras and mmWave radar. We're collecting the data that will teach a low-cost system to identify plastics for autonomous recycling. Before grad school I worked at Genpact on a Google/YouTube operations team, where I learned to meet strict quality standards and trained in generative and agentic AI.

I'm looking for roles in AI/ML engineering, computer vision, data science and software engineering, where I can keep building things that see, measure and decide.
